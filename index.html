About Us

We are a computational cancer genomics lab in the Department of Data Science at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, part of the Hale Center for Pancreatic Cancer Research. Our lab integrates AI, computational biology, genomics and evolution to understand and ultimately alter the molecular processes that initiate and drive cancer. We develop multi-modal foundation models for AI cancer genomics and clinical applications such as early detection, as well as computational frameworks for CRISPR screens, spatial genomics and immunology. We are always looking for new collaborators and passionate researchers to join our team. Please reach out if you are interested in working with us!

Research Areas

GenAI Methods for Tumor Evolution and Early Detection

  • Developing genomics single cell Foundation Models to explain and generate tumor evolution
  • LLM AI approaches for early detection of pancreatic cancer from time-series electronic health record data
Learn More

Spatial Progression of Pancreatic Tumors

  • Spatial transcriptomics & proteomics modeling of pancreatic cancer progression
  • Analysis using clinical trial patient genomic and imaging data
  • Integration of CosMx spatial transcriptomics with computational pathology and foundation models
Learn More

Pancreatic Cancer Genomics

  • Integrative longitudinal immune modeling using paired scRNA-seq and TCR-seq data
  • Cancer surveillance and patient outcome prediction after immunotherapy
  • Drug perturbation analysis through CRISPR screens on KRAS inhibitors
  • Cell surfaceome analysis to identify novel antibody-drug conjugate targets
Learn More

Breast Cancer Genomics

  • Spatial and temporal single cell analyses of breast tumor initiation
  • Study of malignant transformations in breast cancer
  • Clinical response prediction for triple-negative breast cancers treated with antibody-drug conjugates
